The Books

Each book introduces a new plant-world friend who teaches alongside Sally.

Book 1 🌰 🌱
Book One
Sally Finds a Seed
🌰 Meet Sammy the Sunflower Seed

Sally discovers a tiny seed on the ground and wonders — what's hiding inside? Sammy the Sunflower Seed takes her on a journey through the anatomy of a seed and the magic of what it holds.

Kids will learn

  • What a seed is and what's inside it
  • Different types of seeds (big, small, winged, pods)
  • Why seeds are the start of all plant life
  • How to collect and sort seeds
✏️ Activity: Seed Sort & Sketch
Book 2 🌿 🐛
Book Two
Wake Up, Little Seed!
🌿 Meet Greta the Germinating Bean

Sally plants her seed and waits… and waits. Then one morning — a tiny sprout! Greta the Bean explains the incredible process of germination and why different seeds wake up at different speeds.

Kids will learn

  • What germination means
  • The stages: seed → root → sprout → seedling
  • Why some seeds germinate faster than others
  • What conditions seeds need to sprout
✏️ Activity: Germination Jar Experiment
Book 3 ☀️ 🍃
Book Three
Eating Sunshine
🍃 Meet Luma the Leaf

Why do plants need sunlight? Luma the Leaf reveals the secret kitchen inside every leaf — where sunlight, water, and air cook up plant food. Sally is amazed that plants make their own meals!

Kids will learn

  • How photosynthesis works (simply explained)
  • What chlorophyll is and why leaves are green
  • The role of sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide
  • How plants give us the oxygen we breathe
✏️ Activity: Leaf Rubbing + Sun Chart
Book 4 🌸 🐝
Book Four
Hello, Beautiful Flower!
🌸 Meet Flora the First Flower

One day Sally's plant grows a beautiful flower! Flora explains why flowers bloom, how bees help with pollination, and how a flower eventually becomes a new seed — completing the cycle.

Kids will learn

  • Parts of a flower (petals, stamen, pistil)
  • How and why flowers bloom
  • What pollination is and bees' role
  • How flowers turn into fruit and new seeds
✏️ Activity: Flower Dissection & Drawing
Book 5 💧 🌱
Book Five
Dirt, Water & Me
💧 Meet Drip the Water Drop & Sandy the Soil

Sally learns that a healthy plant needs more than sunlight. Drip and Sandy show her what's in soil and how water travels from roots to leaves — and why caring for plants means caring for the earth beneath our feet.

Kids will learn

  • What soil is made of (minerals, nutrients, organisms)
  • How roots drink water and send it up the plant
  • How much water different plants need
  • Composting basics and healthy soil
✏️ Activity: Build a Mini Compost Bin
Book 6 🌍 🌳
Book Six
Plants & Me & the Earth
🌳 Meet Terra the Tree

In the final book, Sally steps back and sees the big picture. Terra the Tree explains how every plant — from tiny grass to giant trees — helps clean air, feed animals, prevent flooding, and keep our planet healthy.

Kids will learn

  • How plants clean the air we breathe
  • How plants feed animals and humans
  • How roots prevent soil erosion
  • Simple ways kids can help plants thrive
✏️ Activity: Plant Your Own Garden Corner

Meet Sally

Sally is curious, kind, and loves to explore. In every Plant & Play book, she opens the story with a question she doesn't know the answer to — and invites readers to discover it alongside her and her new plant-world friend.
